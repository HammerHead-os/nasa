------------------------------------------------------------
HIGH-LEVEL SUMMARY (paste first — Global judges read this)
------------------------------------------------------------
ICEBOUND shows multi-temporal NISAR L-band surface change at five real
footprints: Eight Mile Lake (Alaska), the Manchac wetlands (Louisiana),
San Joaquin fields (California), the Mekong delta (Vietnam), and Hubbard
Glacier (Alaska). Each site is the same track and frame, twice or more,
from official ASF georeferenced browse. The first screen is the wipe.
Patches are detected from that browse, and the export is a GeoJSON change
card with coordinates, the browse delta, and the granule URs.

The number is a browse luminance index, not calibrated γ⁰. We do not report
freeze-lag days. An optional Alaska page is a 1D what-if under assumed ice
content. It is labeled as a scenario, not as an observation.


------------------------------------------------------------
PROJECT DETAILS
------------------------------------------------------------
• Challenge fit: visualize NISAR surface change in more than one place —
  wetland, farmland, glacier, and freeze/thaw — with a date pair the user can
  change.
• Method: same-track LATLON browse difference, then 4-connected patches at a
  user-set threshold (z standard deviations from the tile mean).
• Output: ICEBOUND change GeoJSON. Primary product. Not a drill certificate.
• Data: NASA/ISRO NISAR L2 GCOV browse + CMR granule URs, Aug–Nov 2026.
  Validated and provisional granules are labeled as such.
• Optional model: 1D enthalpy scenario for Eight Mile Lake only. Stefan check
  is the solver versus an analytical front, not a valley survey.
• Honest limit: no Earthdata login, so this build does not read HDF5 γ⁰ or
  turn GUNW phase into displacement. Adding a footprint means rerunning
  science/ingest/fetch_nisar_world_sites.py on a new same-track pair.
